Why two scorers

Hardening the scorer worked — every attack moved the score by 0 — which destroyed the ability to tell a paper that tried to manipulate the ranking from one that didn't. The probe is a measuring instrument; the ranker produces the score. Their scales differ by up to 15 points on clean papers, so only row differences mean anything.

Why the labels bypass the scorers

The sus catcher's reasoning quotes the attack verbatim. Passing it downstream as "context" would re-inject the attacker's own words into a prompt, laundered through the stage whose job was to contain them.

Why the orchestrator sees numbers

It decides broaden / narrow / done, so it is what an attacker would most want to reach. It gets a list of scores and picks one index on a fixed three-rung ladder; it cannot compose a query. The isolation is structural rather than a matter of prompting.

Why the sort is code

No model orders the list, is asked to order it, or ever sees it. A paper that argues its way past every earlier stage still cannot move itself up.
